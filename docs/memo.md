# Can Diversification Offset Qualcomm's Handset Decline?
**Qualcomm Revenue Forecast & Guidance Variance Analysis | FY24–FY27E**
Prepared by Calvin Magsie · October 2026 · Built from public earnings releases

## Bottom Line
Not yet. Automotive and IoT are growing fast, but in the base case they replace only **62%** (FY26E) and **84%** (FY27E) of lost handset revenue. Total QCT + QTL revenue declines **3.3%** in FY26E and **1.5%** in FY27E. The offset arrives only if handset declines are held to roughly **9% or less** in FY27.

## Key Findings

**1. Qualcomm guides conservatively.**
QCT revenue landed at or above the guidance range in all 10 quarters analyzed (3 above, 7 within, 0 below). On average it finished **2.9%** above the guidance midpoint, about **92%** of the way to the top of the range. QTL landed within range every quarter, averaging +2.5%.

**2. The revenue mix is shifting quickly.**

| Fiscal year | Handsets | Automotive | IoT | Auto + IoT share of QCT |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY24 | $24.9B | $2.9B | $5.4B | 25% |
| FY25 | $27.8B | $4.0B | $6.6B | 28% |
| FY26E | $24.0B | $5.7B | $7.2B | 35% |
| FY27E | $21.1B | $7.4B | $7.9B | 42% |

**3. Growth does not yet cover the handset gap (base case).**

| | FY26E vs FY25 | FY27E vs FY26E |
|---|---|---|
| Handset revenue change | −$3.8B | −$2.9B |
| Auto + IoT revenue change | +$2.3B | +$2.4B |
| Offset ratio | 0.62x | 0.84x |
| QCT + QTL revenue growth | −3.3% | −1.5% |

**4. Margins compress during the transition.**
QCT EBT margin falls from 30.4% (FY25) to about 27% (FY26E) and 26% (FY27E). This reflects lower handset volume, memory-cost pressure, and the company's FY26 Q4 margin guidance of 23–25%.

## Recommendations
1. **Track the offset ratio as the primary diversification KPI.** Year-over-year Auto + IoT dollar gains divided by handset dollar losses shows progress more clearly than growth rates, which flatter small bases.
2. **Treat the guidance midpoint as a floor for planning.** Given the consistent upside bias, a midpoint + ~3% planning anchor is more realistic than the midpoint alone.
3. **Watch the handset decline rate as the FY27 swing factor.** The pace of the Apple modem roll-off and Android memory-cost pressure matters more to FY27 revenue than another 10 points of automotive growth.
4. **Monitor QCT margin alongside mix.** Diversification that comes with lower-margin products could offset revenue gains at the EBT line.

## Method & Limitations
- Driver-based forecast: each stream = same quarter prior year × (1 + YoY growth), which preserves seasonality. Drivers are anchored to company guidance and management commentary.
- With 11 quarters of history, a statistical time-series model is not reliable, so forecast drivers are analyst judgment, documented in the model's Assumptions tab.
- The FY26 Q4 base case is calibrated to guidance, so its value is in the stream mix rather than the headline total.
